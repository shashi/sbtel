sbtel

    A system drawn as blocks in levels, from text your agent writes.

    Move around

    • Drag the canvas to pan. Pinch, or ⌘/Ctrl + scroll, to zoom.
    • Click a block's triangle to open or close it.
    • Click an ear to list what the block takes or gives; double-click the block for both.
    • Point at a port, a level's name or a line to read its sketch.

    Edit

    • Select something and choose Edit, or click a sketch you pointed at.
    • Drag a block to move it; drop it between two linked blocks to join them.
    • Drag a selected block's handle onto a neighbour to link them. Select a line and press Delete to cut it.
    • Shift-drag a behavior onto a block, or a shape onto an ear, to attach it.

    Comment

    • Select a block or a line and press C, or ⌘/Ctrl-click it.
    • Drop a file on a block or into a comment to attach it.
