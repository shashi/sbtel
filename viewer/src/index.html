sbtel
  • sbtel is a language to design, understand, and collaborate on a system's architecture with AI. This page draws a system as blocks in levels, from text you can edit.
  • Drag the canvas to pan. Pinch, or ⌘/Ctrl + scroll, to zoom.
  • Click a block to select it. Drag one of its handles onto another block in the same level to link them.
  • Click a line and press Delete to cut it.
  • Click the ear above or below a block to open its inputs or outputs. Double-click a block to open both.
  • Point at a port in a drawer to see its sketch and outline every block it can talk to; click it to edit the port. The arrow goes to the type's card.
  • Point at a level's name, or a line between adjacent blocks, to read its sketch; click to edit it.
  • Drag a block to move it (on a touch screen, tap it first, then drag it). Drop it between two linked blocks to join their group.
  • Shift-drag a behavior onto a block, or a shape onto an ear, to attach it.
  • Select something and choose Edit to rename it or change its type and sketch.
  • ⌘/Ctrl-click a block or line to comment on it.
